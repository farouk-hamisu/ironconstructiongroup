Company Overview

A single team for build and power

Iron Construction Group was founded in 1999 with a simple conviction: construction and energy are two halves of the same job — and both deserve engineering discipline.

Today we operate as a national contractor with a full in-house team of structural engineers, project managers, licensed electricians, solar technicians and master tradesmen. That integration is our edge: one accountable team from foundation to first watt produced.

Our Approach

A disciplined process from concept to closeout

Predictable delivery doesn't happen by accident. It happens when engineering, estimating and field execution work as one system.

02

Engineered Execution
